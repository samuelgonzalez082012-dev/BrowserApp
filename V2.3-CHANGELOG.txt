MINIMAL CHROMIUM BROWSER — V2.3 CHANGELOG
Media & Downloads

NEW
- Added a bounded FIFO download scheduler with a default concurrency of three
  active transfers and a hard maximum of ten.
- Excess downloads are paused and represented as Queued until a slot is free.
- Completed, cancelled and non-resumable interrupted transfers release their
  slot and promote the oldest queued transfer.
- Manually pausing a transfer frees a slot for queued work.
- Resuming a paused/interrupted transfer respects the concurrency limit and
  returns it to the queue when all slots are occupied.
- Queued downloads can be cancelled from the Downloads panel.

ENGINEERING
- Queue state is isolated in download-queue.js and does not expose Electron
  DownloadItem objects to the renderer.
- Added node:test coverage for FIFO promotion, pause/resume, bounded limits,
  concurrency changes, duplicate IDs and unknown jobs.

LIMITATIONS
- The queue is currently in-memory. A browser restart cannot resume an active
  Electron DownloadItem; persisted records remain subject to Electron's native
  interrupted-download behavior.
- Download concurrency is configurable from the Downloads panel (1–10); the
  default is three. Lowering the limit does not interrupt already-running files.
- Persisted queued/paused/in-progress records are normalized to Interrupted at
  startup because Electron DownloadItem instances cannot survive app restarts.
- Throughput graphs, scheduling, duplicate detection, file integrity metadata,
  advanced history search and global media controls remain future V2.3 work.

VALIDATION
- Seven isolated download-queue unit tests passed under Node.js.
- Full Electron runtime download testing is still required.
